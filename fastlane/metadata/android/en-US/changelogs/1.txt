First release.

* Dock-first redesigned UI: floating bottom dock, animated screens, live
  transfer telemetry with speed readout and progress ring around Send
* Device discovery over Wi-Fi (multicast + direct by address)
* File transfers both ways, byte-identical, over mutual TLS
* Interrupted transfers resume instead of restarting from zero
* Crush & send (optional): text-like files are gzip'd on the wire, unpacked
  automatically by Flit receivers — other clients still get a valid gzip
* Received files stream straight into Downloads/Flit, your default folder, or
  a per-device folder for trusted devices — no double copies
* Text notes share the same secure wire as files (share sheet or in-app)
* Send-later queue: a send waiting on an offline device completes on return
* Peer fingerprints shown and pinned; optional trusted-senders-only gate
* Hidden mode: stay off other devices' lists until you send first
* Material You theming with light/dark/auto
* Rename your device, start-on-boot toggle, battery-rationing opt-out
* Received ledger: tap to open, re-share, clear
* Foreground service keeps transfers alive with the screen off
* Quick settings tile for one-tap sharing
* Adaptive app icon with themed (monochrome) support
