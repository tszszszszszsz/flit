Works when Wi‑Fi doesn't: with no network between the phones, the roster
keeps working — discovery and the exchange ride Bluetooth at exactly that
moment, the same secure protocol, the same list. No new settings; the only
prompts are the system's own, each asked once per device.

Also: the send-later queue keeps its promise end to end (a second share
joins the waiting batch instead of failing; a flush broken by the peer
walking out keeps its row; completed batches release their storage
grants), and a start-time sweep reclaims disk from staged copies a
previous process left behind.
